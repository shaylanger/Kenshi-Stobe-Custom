## New bugs
34. **Late payment verification leaves her side unsent (bug 29 gap).** Payment evidence arrives through the game event after the chat reply, so `stobeNegAttachPendingForChat` at the end of chat.php runs too early. The settle directive then waits for her next chat line. Fix idea: when a payment verification queues a settle directive outside a chat request, push it straight away (ScriptQueue), or have the DLL or a tick drain directives. The duplicate settle queue at +48 s should be deduped as well.
35. **Speech-amount rewrite replaced a whole unrelated reply.** Her reply (decision NONE) talked about owing the tobacco and said "three hundred cats just to look?". `stobeDealProgressAmountCheck` rewrote the whole line to "You've paid me all 200 Cats." Her real answer was lost.
36. **Offer detector misses "Four hundred then. You take off your Iron Hat… Deal?"** A number with no "cats", and no offer phrase, means negotiation isn't active. She then took the hat off *for free* (UnequipItem with no deal recorded). Fix idea: treat "<number> … deal?" or "<number> then/it is" as an offer, especially right after she named a price.
37. **COUNTER with empty deal_terms.** She countered "make it four hundred" but sent `deal_terms:""`. It was correctly rejected (`invalid_terms_json`), but her counter was lost. Consider a one-shot retry or extracting the amount from speech.
38. **Prompt lists "Shay | squadmate"** in Nearby People Awareness for Malzin, who isn't in Shay's squad.
- Note: straight after `stobe-reset-npc`, her relationship was back to "Resentful (rival) | refuses katana sale" after one line (the relationship eval re-ran). Expected, but it means "reset" doesn't give a neutral start.
- Tobacco hand-over was `dropped_at_feet=1` (not in Shay's inventory), with the known round-9 behaviour.
- Runtime: PocketTTS 8024 still unreachable (falls back).

## New bugs (Part B)
39. **`@help` stripped by the action normalizer.** `normalizeActionTagToken`'s ATTACK branch ran the argument through `$sanitizeInlineText`, which removes '@', so `shay@help` became `shayhelp` (target unresolved, no fight, no gang). Bug 27's fix never reached the game. **Fixed in round 14** and verified in fight 2.
40. **Stand-down misses faction-mates who join after her STOP_ATTACK.** Her stop ran 1 s before the gang engaged (`speaker_faction_members_stopped=0`), and nothing re-stands them down; the truce only covers Malzin↔Shay. Fix idea: while a combat deal's truce is active (and for about 20 s after), re-apply the stand-down to her faction-mates who attack the player, or delay/re-issue the faction stop when a `@help` fight is underway.
41. **Payment deadline counts real time while the game is paused.** The 1-minute pay window expired during Shay's pause → false BREACHED_PLAYER plus a queued revenge `ATTACK@shay`. The deadline should use game time (gamets), or the DLL's pause state should hold it.
42. **She claims payment she never got.** After the false breach, her reply said "Cats received. I'll call them off", while the ledger said UNMET and her actions were `STOP_FOLLOW@`, `ATTACK@shay`. Words and actions contradict; the prompt must reflect a breach, and a breach attack shouldn't ride on a conciliatory line.
- Note: a payment executes in the same step as "Here's …" (fight 1: before her ACCEPT). By design, but a REJECT after a pay-first line relies on the refund path, which is still untested.
- Note: stale unconsumed directives in the DB (ids 2–29, old); harmless (the attach window is 45 s / 600 s for refund).
