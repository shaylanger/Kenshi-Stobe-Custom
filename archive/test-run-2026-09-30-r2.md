## New findings
28. **"Name your price" doesn't start negotiation.** `stobeNegLooksLikeSocialOffer("Then name your price for taking off the Black Rag Shirt…")` is false, so her "Twelve thousand, all of it up front" was never recorded as a counter.
29. **Accept + pay in one line: her side waits for the next line.** "Eight thousand, deal. Here's eight thousand cats." The payment verified, but the settle directive (UNEQUIP) was queued after her reply went out and sat at SETTLE_QUEUED (>60 s) until the next line ("Go ahead then."). Same family as K4.
30. **Words agree, decision doesn't.** Sandals: she said "Fine - but the coin lands in my palm first…", but `deal_decision` was NONE, so nothing was recorded until I asked again. The katana deal also came back COUNTERED with identical terms instead of ACCEPT.
31. **Wrong term type.** "Take off your Black Rag Shirt" was recorded as GIVE_ITEM (hand to Shay) instead of UNEQUIP_ITEM. Restating fixed it.
32. **She disarmed for money with no real trust.** 10,000 Cats to stow her Chisa Katana: accepted ("you've been good for it so far"; she refused at 3,000). Her relationship had been reset at the start of the run. No server rule protects an NPC's own weapon; only the LLM's judgment. Also unknown: whether Kenshi's AI re-equips a weapon from the pack or picks one up off the floor in combat. **Proposed:** a hard rule. No UNEQUIP/GIVE/DROP of the weapon she's using unless (a) she's surrendering in a fight, (b) trust is above a threshold, or (c) she has another weapon; plus a prompt line ("in Kenshi your weapon is your life"). **Shay chose (a) + (b); a spare doesn't count. Fixed in server round 11** (trust = affinity >= `NEG_WEAPON_TRUST_MIN`, default 56 "Fond"; enforced in the prompt, deal capture and reply actions). Not retested in game yet.
33. **A stop deal right after an attack was recorded as social.** Her combat flag lags about 1 s or more, so `stobeDealKindFor` said social. Fixed in **server round 10c** (`patch_round10c.py`, live + ss-merge): she counts as fighting the player if her combat flag says so, OR she has a personal-fight registration under 60 s old, OR she attacked the player within 60 s (eventlog). Verified in the 2nd stop above.
