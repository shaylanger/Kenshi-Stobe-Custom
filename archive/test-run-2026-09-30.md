## Fix status (round 7, deployed 2026-09-30)
Server (`pending-fixes/patch_round7.py`, live + ss-merge, live immediately):
- **Bugs 7 + 11, root cause:** `resolved_at` was written as UTC (`gmdate`) while the DB clock is Europe/Madrid (UTC+2), so every "finished in the last N minutes" check missed. That let duplicate deals through (7), and it made refunds look like unpaid gifts, which then got blocked (11). Fixed: stamped with `NOW()`.
- **Bug 8:** a knock-on of 7 (the stuck duplicate took the "deal in progress" slot and silently swallowed the new deal). Now logged as `Negotiation capture ignored: another deal is still being performed`. **Design limitation remains:** a second, different deal is still ignored while one is being performed.
- **Bug 1:** an NPC-side IMPOSSIBLE term now resolves the deal IMPOSSIBLE (refund) before the player-unmet check.
- **Bug 3 (server):** player money falls back to conf_opts `PLAYER_CATS`.
- **Bug 4:** "take the/it/…" no longer counts as a hand-over after "you".
- **Bug 6:** the ceasefire speech rewrite only applies to combat/surrender deals.
- **Bug 9:** the generic "one food you carry" hand-over is skipped when a deal names what's owed.
- **Bug 10:** the model left out `to`; it's now filled with the other side.

DLL (`pending-fixes/patch_dll_round7.py`, needs a Kenshi restart):
- **Bug 3, root cause:** upstream 49ce420 dropped the player Cats and squad sync when it moved world reads into the update hook, so `PLAYER_CATS` was only ever set by `give_cats`. Restored in the update tick (every 2 s).
- **Bug 3 (DLL):** a player GIVE_CATS larger than the purse is now refused, not clamped.

Not fixed yet: **bug 12** (speech/action consistency guard), **bug 5** (NPC money never stored, so verification relies on the game's execution report), **bug 2** (check once bug 1 is live).

`stobe-tests`: 52 passed, 0 failed, 7 known pre-existing.

## Bugs found
1. **Impossible deal stays open, and a refusal breaches it.** deal-eb8d3e1b7fe5e5ed5c6e1f8c (3,000 Cats for Malzin's Iron Hat) had its NPC term marked IMPOSSIBLE on 2026-09-29 (`action_blocked_by_policy`), but the deal stayed open for ~24 h. D2's "no way I'm paying for that" was then taken as refusing it: BREACHED_PLAYER, and Malzin drew her weapon. Expected: a deal with an IMPOSSIBLE NPC term closes as failed (with a refund of anything paid, as in E5), and a refusal on a deal the NPC couldn't fulfil shouldn't count as the player's breach.
2. **Why was the hat hand-over blocked by policy on 09-29?** Probably the equipped-gear gift rule (J4). Check that the round-6 fix covers this case.

3. **Overpayment is clamped to the whole purse.** Two layers failed:
   - Server: `negotiation_voice.php` only returns `insufficient_cats` when the player's money is *known* (`stobeNegMoney`: `metadata.money` on the player's row). It went through, so the stored value was missing or stale. Check how the 1.3.1 DLL syncs player Cats (`SyncPlayerCatsValue`) and whether it reaches `metadata.money`.
   - DLL: `Functions.cpp` ~8721, `ACT_GIVE_CATS` does `amt = min(taskValue, actorMoney)`. For a *player* payment this should refuse, not clamp: transfer nothing and report the shortage back so the NPC is told.

4. **An offer phrased as a question pays immediately.** "The vodka and 3000 cats, and you take the hat off. Deal?" → `Voice hand-over dispatched` (GIVE_CATS 3000 + GIVE_ITEM Vodka) before any agreement. The hand-over detector in `negotiation_voice.php` should treat "X and you do Y. Deal?" as an offer, not a hand-over. (It worked out because she accepted, but a refusal would have left her with the goods.)
5. **Verification evidence shows zero deltas.** deal-0e60aff43a2c2763356c6f64: `npc_money_delta: 0` and `npc_count_delta: 0`, yet both are VERIFIED from `executed`. The server isn't seeing the NPC's money or inventory change. This is probably linked to bug 3 (stale money/inventory sync with the 1.3.1 DLL).

6. **The ceasefire guard rewrites social counter-offers.** `Negotiation speech implied ceasefire without ACCEPT; rewritten` fired on a *social* COUNTER ("Make it fifteen hundred and a decent meal, and you've got a deal.") and replaced the whole line with "Not until you meet my terms." The player never hears the counter terms. The guard should only apply to combat deals, or at least keep the counter.
7. **Duplicate deal after completion re-counts the same payment.** Right after deal-68e177c1… completed, her closing line ("Alright, you held up your end…") recorded deal-75f34458e9a57529a76c29c2 with the same terms. The **same** 1,500 + bread show as VERIFIED on it too, and the sandals term was re-dispatched (they're already off). This is the same family as J1/E8/I8. **Confirmed:** at 17:38:16 the duplicate went IMPOSSIBLE and queued a refund `GIVE_CATS@shay@1500` for money that went to the real deal. That's a free-Cats exploit.

8. **A counter and acceptance weren't recorded as a deal.** At 17:38:08 Malzin's reply was tagged `deal=COUNTER` (5,000 + 10 dried meat for the rag shirt), but no contract was recorded. My "Fine… Deal." got a reply with no deal tag and no contract. So the demand for a specific item wasn't enforced, and the next "here's your food" handed over an unrelated food (E3).
9. **The hand-over picks any food when "food" isn't pinned down.** "Here's your food" with no deal on record → GIVE_ITEM Dried Grand Fish. With a deal demanding a specific item, it should hand over only that item. Without a deal, "your food" is ambiguous, and handing over nothing or asking seems safer.

10. **`invalid_recipient` throws away the whole deal.** `negotiation_phase1.php:33` requires `to` ∈ {npc, player} on GIVE_CATS/GIVE_ITEM terms. The model evidently wrote a name (same family as J8, where it wrote the player's name for "player"). The deal was dropped, and the player's 2,000 prepayment was orphaned. Normalise the NPC's and player's names (and "me"/"you") to npc/player before validating, not reject.

11. **Refund directives are consumed but never executed.** Directive #15 (`refund GIVE_CATS@shay@1500`) was `consumed=yes attached_to_chat` on Malzin's 17:38:29 reply, but that reply didn't mention it and no GIVE_CATS from Malzin ran in the game. Here that blocked the bug-7 exploit, but a legitimate E5 refund would be lost the same way. Check how attached directive actions get into the reply's action list.

12. **Speech promises an action that isn't sent.** The LLM returned `"action": "Talk"` with "Let me put the sandals back on." (output_from_llm.log ~line 9838), so nothing was equipped. Add a consistency guard: if the line announces equipping or unequipping an item but the action is Talk, either fill in the action (when the item is unambiguous) or rewrite the line.

13. **An NPC with a full pack can't take clothes off.** `UNEQUIP_ITEM no carried section has room` (KenshiFP). The deal correctly ends IMPOSSIBLE, but she had already said "let me get it off". Options: drop the item at her feet, or check pack room before she agrees.
14. **Stray bytes on the end of chat events.** Some player lines reach the server with binary junk after the text ("Deal?" → "Deal6??…", "Hickeys. (talking to: Keys)7"). The server sanitizes them, but a trailing "?" can be lost. This is an older DLL string/buffer issue, not the test inbox.

15. **One-slot mailboxes were overwritten.** `unequip_item.request` and `stobe_action.request` writers renamed straight over a pending request, so the second of two back-to-back actions replaced the first. Fixed in round 7e (wait up to 2 s for the slot).
16. **UNEQUIP verification ignored the item.** Any successful unequip by that NPC verified every UNEQUIP term, which made I4 COMPLETE with the shirt still on. Fixed in round 7e (matched per item).
17. **The model writes deal_terms as text, not JSON** (both the prompt's summary format and free prose). Round 7c+7f parse both, strictly (all or nothing).

18. **One payment counted for two deals.** The pre-deal lookback stopped 2 s after the previous deal but was searched with 3 s of slack, so I4's 2500 (paid 18:57:33.9) verified the tobacco deal agreed 35 s later. Fixed in round 7g (6 s margin).
19. **Bug 4 variant.** "…, I pay you 300 cats. Deal?" paid immediately ("i pay you" cue; "Deal?" judged as a separate sentence). Fixed in round 7h: a message ending with an agreement question is an offer unless it contains an explicit "here's / here you go".

20. **The I10 breach started a real fight, and the truce didn't stop it (safety stop).** After "I'm not gonna pay you" (13:01:52, "…carve it out of your hide") Malzin attacked; combat ran 102 s, from ~13:01:54 to 13:03:36, and ended in Shay's defeat. The late 300 payment made a combat truce deal and KenshiFP ran `STOP_FIGHT … result=ok` at 13:03:14 plus 3 reapplies, but attack events continued (13:03:17) until Shay went down. So the stop is reported ok but doesn't hold against an NPC in a personal fight: the same family as the old F1/J8 failures. Also a process miss: the tester (Claude) didn't check combat events after I10 and kept going.
    - Related: her REJECT reply at 13:02:22 carried an `Attack` action. A breach reaction should be proportionate (threaten, deadline) before attacking, per J2's design.
