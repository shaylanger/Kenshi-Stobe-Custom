## Bugs
- **71** LOOT_AREA does not match Sorth's dead body (`dead=1 valid=0`, both C1 and C2). `stg_valid_downed` rejects him: either `isPlayerCharacter`, or the name at +0x18 not containing "sorth" for a dead char. Fix: log per-char name/dead/prone/isplayer in the loot scan, then fix the match. Also `stobe-say state Sorth` → "target not found" (test inbox can't see dead NPCs).
- **72** Goal report not spoken: C2 COMPLETE → server `Goal report queued` + `Bored event taken by negotiation directive`, but Stobe.dll dispatched `mode=director` and the server logged `Director scene failed: No eligible Director cast`; Malzin said nothing (bugs 65/66 area). Confirm in R1.
- **73** Malzin talks about dead Sorth as alive ("still breathing", "lost most of his blood") while he is Dead. The server prompt doesn't tell the LLM that a nearby NPC is dead (roster lists "Sorth [Dust Bandit]" only). Fix: mark dead/unconscious NPCs in the roster/context.
- **74** (minor) In-game job list doesn't refresh when GOAL_JOB adds/removes a job; it updates only after selecting another character and back. Fix: trigger the job-list UI refresh after add/remove.
- **75** Walk-back silently does nothing for 180 s, then gives up. In `sgr_tick` every early `continue` is silent (actor/player not found, `actor==player`, position failed). Likely cause: Shay had Malzin **selected** (checking her job list), so `first_player_char` = Malzin = actor. Her goal orders were also never cleared, so she stayed at the processor. Fix: walk back to the nearest other squad member when the selected one is the actor; log why each tick is skipped; clear her orders on start.
- **76** A direct work order ("Make 3 steel bars") became a question ("where's the iron coming from?") with no goal queued. Server guard: a plain player imperative "make/build N X" to a faction member must emit WORK_GOAL; the planner reports BLOCKED itself.
- **77** "Put them back in the chest" after fetching two items → one STORE goal (only the last item, Mead). "Them" must expand to every item the last fetch brought (bug 60's fix covered FETCH only). Also check STORE walks to the chest (it completed 2 ms after accept).
- **78** At high game speed she idles a lot between steps: planner timers are real time (`WGP_TICK_MS 1000`, `STG_TICK_MS 800`, power wait 30 s, stalls, walk-back give-up 180 s), so at 50x each step costs ~50+ game seconds. Fix: tick every ~1000/speed ms (floor ~50 ms), measure waits in game time, chain steps that don't need waiting.
- (idea) Goals kept working her while starving until she passed out. Consider pausing the goal / eating from storage when hungry. Watch script: also pause on `[EVENT] knockout` of Shay/Malzin.
- **72 (more)** Goal reports aren't delivered at all: R1 retry and R2 got `flag consumed, initiative turn armed` and then nothing (no dispatch, no `Goal report queued`). The flag is consumed right at goal end, not when she's back within 70.
